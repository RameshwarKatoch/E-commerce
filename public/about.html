Our Mission

Bringing Nature Closer to You

Green Bloom was born from a simple belief: everyone deserves a connection with nature, no matter where they live. In the heart of urban India, we saw concrete jungles yearning for a touch of green — and we knew we could make a difference.

We partner with certified organic nurseries across India to bring you the healthiest, most vibrant plants — each one hand-inspected and lovingly packaged before it reaches your doorstep.

Our Story

The Journey of Green Bloom

2020

The Seed Was Planted

Founded during the lockdown, Green Bloom started as a small balcony nursery selling plants to neighbours through WhatsApp.

2021

First 1,000 Customers

Word spread fast. Our monstera went viral on Instagram, and we hit our first thousand happy plant parents.

2022

Nationwide Shipping

We partnered with specialized plant logistics to ship live plants across India with a 98% survival rate.

2023

500+ Varieties

Our collection expanded to include rare exotics, succulents, outdoor flowering plants, and bonsai trees.

2025

10,000+ Plant Parents

Today, Green Bloom serves thousands of homes and offices, with a community that grows every single day.
