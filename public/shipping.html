🌡 How We Pack Your Plants

Every plant is packaged using our proprietary Moisture-Lock™ system — breathable boxes with water-retention gel packs that keep soil moist during transit. Each pot is cushioned with recycled paper to prevent shifting.

📦 Order Tracking

Once your order is shipped, you'll receive:

  • An email with your tracking number
  • WhatsApp updates at every stage
  • Live delivery tracking link

🔄 Returns & Replacements

If your plant arrives damaged or unhealthy:

  • Report within 48 hours of delivery with photos
  • We'll send a free replacement — no questions asked
  • Refunds processed within 5–7 business days

🌿 30-Day Plant Guarantee

All Green Bloom plants come with our 30-day survival guarantee. If your plant doesn't thrive within 30 days despite following our care guide, we'll replace it at no charge.
